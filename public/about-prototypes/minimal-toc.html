About The Book Prize Index

A free public catalog of prize-recognized books, built for slower and more deliberate discovery.

Why

I made this because I wanted a better way to find books that had been taken seriously by readers, critics, librarians, juries, and prize committees, but were not necessarily being pushed back at me by an algorithm.

Prize records are imperfect. They are not a canon and they are not a substitute for taste. But they are useful traces of attention, and those traces become more interesting when they can be searched together.

Scope

The current focus is nonfiction: history, biography, memoir, criticism, science and nature writing, politics, reportage, and general nonfiction.

The index connects award records to books, authors, subjects, imprints, publishers, and source links.

Method

Award records are the foundation. Each public record should begin with a source: prize, year, category, status, title, author, and URL when one is available.

  • official award archives are preferred
  • secondary sources are marked and revisited
  • generated metadata is treated as evidence, not authority

Limits

This is still a working index. Some prizes have deep archives; others have only winners or recent shortlists. Some books have complete metadata; others still need review.

The goal is to make uncertainty visible rather than smooth it away.

Corrections

Missing sources, duplicate books, wrong authors, and confused imprints are all useful to know about. The project should improve through source-backed correction.
